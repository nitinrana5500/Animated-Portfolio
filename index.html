My Process

A clear path.
A considered outcome.

Great work starts with understanding. Here is how an idea takes shape, one collaborative step at a time.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Explore your goals, audience, and inspiration. Define the problem and agree on a focused creative brief.

  2. 02

    Design

    Translate the brief into visual directions. Explore ideas with a clear reason behind every design decision.

  3. 03

    Refine

    Discuss what works, gather feedback, and develop the chosen direction until the details feel right.

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Prepare the agreed assets and guidance so you can put your new design to work with confidence.

A Few Answers

Before we
make a start.

A little clarity on the process, the scope, and what working together looks like.

What kinds of projects can we work on?

Brand strategy, visual identities, packaging, and creative direction. A project can focus on a single touchpoint or bring several pieces together into a consistent brand experience.

What do you need to get started?

A short introduction to your business, what you want to achieve, and any existing brand materials is a useful starting point. References, a target timeline, and a budget range help shape the brief.

How is the project timeline decided?

The timeline depends on the scope, deliverables, and feedback stages. These are discussed at the beginning so the schedule reflects what the project actually needs.

How does feedback work?

Feedback is gathered at agreed milestones, with each round focused on the goals in the brief. The number of review rounds is defined as part of the project scope.

What will I receive at the end?

Final deliverables are agreed before design begins. Depending on the project, these may include logo files, brand guidelines, packaging artwork, or visual direction documents.
